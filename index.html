2 Answer key

QAnswerPointsMatch

Value: 1/2, 0.5 and 2/4 all count. Exact form: the answer must be written the way the key is (6/8 does not count for 3/4).

3 Photos

  • 5 to 10 slips per photo.
  • Lay them flat on a dark or colored surface, not overlapping, with a gap between them.
  • Good light, shot straight down, every slip fully in the frame.
